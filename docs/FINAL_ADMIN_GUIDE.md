# Administrator Guide

A simple walkthrough of everyday admin tasks. For first-time setup of a
brand-new deployment (creating the very first admin account), see
`ADMIN_SETUP_GUIDE.md` instead — this guide assumes the portal is
already live and you can already log in.

## Login

Go to your school's portal URL, enter your email and password, and tap
**Sign in**. Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?** on the
login screen and follow the emailed link.

## Add staff / Add teachers

**Users → Invite user** (or **Staff → Add staff**). Enter their name,
email, and role (Admin, Teacher, or Staff). They'll get an email to set
their own password.

## Add students

**Students → Add student**. Fill in their details and assign them to a
class.

## Create classes

**Classes → Add class**. Name it and pick the academic session it
belongs to.

## Add subjects

**Subjects → Add subject**. Subjects are shared across classes — create
once, then attach to whichever classes teach it.

## Assign teachers

**Teacher Assignments → New assignment**. Pick the teacher, subject,
class, and session. This is what lets a teacher take attendance and
enter results for that class — a teacher only sees classes they're
assigned to.

## Record attendance

**Attendance**, pick a class and date, mark each student present or
absent, save. (Teachers do this for their own assigned classes;
admins can view or correct any class.)

## Enter results

**Results**, pick a class, subject, and term, enter CA and exam scores
per student. Grades calculate automatically from the grading scale set
in Settings.

## Upload documents

**Documents → Upload document**. Give it a title, choose who can see it
(everyone, teachers only, staff only, or one specific class), and
attach the file.

## Publish announcements

**Announcements → New announcement**. Write it, choose the audience the
same way as documents, and optionally set a future publish date or an
expiry date after which it stops showing.

## Manage users

**Users** — see everyone's role, status, and last login in one place.
Tap into any user to activate/deactivate their account or change their
role. (You can't change your own role — ask another admin if that's
ever needed.)

## Update school settings

**Settings** — school name, logo, address, and contact details at the
top; academic sessions, terms, and the grading scale below. Only one
session and one term can be "current" at a time — that's what teachers
and staff see by default across the portal.
